Grammar or rewrite result looks wrong?

Try a complete sentence or paragraph with enough context. AI output can vary, so review suggestions before using them.

Dictionary result needs context?

Words can have multiple meanings and regional usages. Review examples and surrounding context before choosing a definition or synonym.

Recent work missing?

Check whether app data was cleared, the app was reinstalled or device migration/backup behavior changed local application data.

Ads or consent issue?

Advertising behavior can depend on network status, region, consent settings, Google Play services and the current AdMob configuration.

App crash or performance issue?

Restart the App, confirm you have the current Google Play version and send support the device/app details needed to reproduce the problem.
